## 1. Typography

### Font Hierarchy

Baldor uses three proprietary typefaces. Since these are licensed fonts that may not be available on all systems, the skill must specify fallbacks that preserve the visual weight and character of each role.

#### Primary — Slide Headers
- **Font:** Ruder Plakat LL VIP High 900
- **Character:** Ultra-bold condensed display face. High visual impact. Wide letter spacing.
- **Fallback chain:** Impact → Arial Black → Helvetica Bold
- **Usage:** Slide titles, major section headings, cover slide titles
- **Case:** Sentence case OR ALL CAPS — both are acceptable
- **Sizes:** 36–60pt for slide titles; 28–36pt for section headers
- **Colors:** Cream (#FAF8F3) on dark backgrounds; Evergreen (#20491D) on light backgrounds

#### Secondary — Subheads & Body
- **Font:** Herbik Regular / Herbik Italic
- **Character:** Warm, slightly editorial serif-like sans. Readable at body sizes.
- **Fallback chain:** Georgia → Cambria → Times New Roman
- **Usage:** Subheadings, body paragraphs, longer copy blocks, quotes
- **Case:** Standard sentence case
- **Sizes:** 18–24pt for subheads; 14–16pt for body; 20–28pt for quotes
- **Colors:** Charcoal (#2C3133) for body; Evergreen (#20491D) for subheads

#### Tertiary — Bullets, Captions, Details
- **Font:** FT Polar Semibold
- **Character:** Geometric sans-serif with strong presence at small sizes
- **Fallback chain:** Trebuchet MS → Calibri → Arial
- **Usage:** Bullet points, captions, metadata, footer text, labels, sub-heads
- **Case:** ALWAYS ALL CAPS in PowerPoint presentations
- **Sizes:** 10–14pt for bullets/captions; 8–9pt for footer; 12–16pt for labels
- **Colors:** Shale (#9A9D9E) for captions; Charcoal (#2C3133) for bullets; Lime (#B3CF44) for labels on dark backgrounds

### Color Pairing Rules

- **Dark bg (Evergreen/Olive/Charcoal):** Title text in Cream, subtitle in Lime, body in Cream/Lt Cream
- **Light bg (Cream/Wheat):** Title in Evergreen, body in Charcoal, accents in Lime
- **Never:** White (#FFFFFF) as primary background (use Cream); off-brand colors; tertiary as bg fills

## 3. Slide Layout Patterns

### Cover Slide
- **Background:** Evergreen (#20491D) or Olive (#20280B) on the LEFT HALF
- **Right half:** Full-bleed food photography (seasonal produce)
- **Title:** Large display text (Ruder/Impact) in Cream, left-aligned on the green half
- **Subtitle:** Presenter name + title in Herbik/Georgia Italic, Lime color
- **Logo:** Baldor oval wordmark positioned on or near the photo

### Section Divider
- **Background:** Solid Evergreen
- **Title:** Centered, large, Cream text (Ruder/Impact)
- **Optional subtitle:** Lime (Herbik/Georgia)

### Content Slide (default)
- **Background:** Cream (#FAF8F3)
- **Title:** Top-left, Evergreen, Ruder/Impact, 36-44pt
- **Body:** Left-aligned, Charcoal, Herbik/Georgia, 14-16pt
- **Bullets:** FT Polar/Trebuchet, ALL CAPS, 11-13pt
- **Footer:** "CONFIDENTIAL & PROPRIETARY" centered bottom, 8pt, Shale

### Two-Column Content
- **Left column:** Text content (title + bullets or paragraphs)
- **Right column:** Image placeholder (Wheat fill, "PLACE IMAGE HERE" label)
- **Divider:** No visible line — use whitespace

### Stats/Data Slide
- **Large numbers:** 60-72pt, Evergreen or Lime, Ruder/Impact
- **Labels below numbers:** FT Polar/Trebuchet, ALL CAPS, 12pt, Charcoal
- **Supporting text:** Herbik/Georgia, 14pt, Charcoal

### Quote/Testimonial
- **Background:** Wheat (#D4D0C5) or Cream
- **Quote text:** Herbik/Georgia Italic, 20-24pt, Charcoal or Evergreen
- **Opening quotation mark:** Optional large decorative mark in Lime
- **Attribution:** FT Polar/Trebuchet, ALL CAPS, 11pt, prefixed with em-dash

### Timeline/Growth
- **Background:** Cream
- **Timeline line:** Lime (#B3CF44) horizontal or vertical
- **Year markers:** Ruder/Impact, Evergreen
- **Descriptions:** FT Polar/Trebuchet, ALL CAPS, Charcoal

### Closing Slide
- **Background:** Evergreen or Olive, sometimes with food photo bleed
- **"Thank You.":** Large centered text, Cream, Ruder/Impact
- **Copyright:** "(C) 2024 BALDOR SPECIALTY FOODS" small, bottom, Cream

## 4. Brand Identity Elements

### Logo Usage
- The Baldor logo is an **oval containing the word "Baldor" in script**
- Appears on cover slides and optionally on closing slides
- Do NOT generate the logo — use a placeholder or reference the user's logo file
- Acceptable logo backgrounds: over food photography, on Evergreen, on Cream with adequate contrast

### Footer Convention
Every slide except cover and closing includes:
```
CONFIDENTIAL & PROPRIETARY
```
Centered at bottom. FT Polar/Trebuchet. ALL CAPS. 8pt. Color: Shale (#9A9D9E).

Page numbers appear bottom-right in matching style.

### Brand Voice (for auto-generated placeholder text)
- Professional yet passionate about food
- "Quality you can trust. Service that delivers."
- Authoritative but warm — like a trusted culinary partner
- Avoid consumer-facing casualness; this is B2B foodservice

## 5. Do's and Don'ts

### DO
- Use Cream (#FAF8F3) as default background, NOT white
- Use the font fallback chain if proprietary fonts aren't available
- Apply ALL CAPS to all FT Polar / tertiary font text
- Include "CONFIDENTIAL & PROPRIETARY" footer on content slides
- Use food photography placeholders where images belong
- Maintain generous whitespace — Baldor aesthetic is clean and premium
- Use Lime (#B3CF44) as the primary accent for emphasis

### DON'T
- Use any colors outside the Baldor theme palette
- Use tertiary colors as full slide backgrounds
- Mix fonts outside the three-tier hierarchy
- Use white (#FFFFFF) as a slide background (use Cream)
- Add decorative borders, gradients, or drop shadows
- Use clip art or generic icons — Baldor is photography-driven
- Place text directly on busy photographic backgrounds without a color panel behind it
- Forget the CONFIDENTIAL footer
